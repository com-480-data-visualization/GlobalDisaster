Global Natural Disasters

Data source: EM-DAT International Disaster Database

Getting started

Use the two buttons at the top left to activate the visualization modes:

  • Disasters on Globe — activates the map visualization with country coloring.
  • Disasters in Countries — enables clicking on countries to see detailed statistics.

Choosing what to display

The panel on the left lets you select up to two features at once:

  • The color tag shows which feature drives the country color (choropleth).
  • The shape tag shows which feature drives bubble size — activating a second numeric feature switches to bubble map mode.

Bubble map mode

When two numeric features are selected, proportional bubbles appear over each country. You can:

  • Hover a bubble to highlight it and see its value.
  • Click a bubble to open the country detail panel (requires Disasters in Countries to be active).
  • Click on a continent background (between bubbles) to open the continent bubble panel.

Filtering data

  • Use the timeline slider at the bottom to filter by year range. Click the year label to jump to a specific year.
  • Use Filter by disaster type in the left panel to include only certain disaster categories.

Continent panel

Clicking a continent background opens a side panel showing all countries in that continent as proportional bubbles. Hover any bubble to see the country's value.
